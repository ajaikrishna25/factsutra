# FactSutra Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 10 November 2026_

FactSutra is an informational news Page covering India and the world.

## What the FactSutra app does
The FactSutra app is used only to publish and manage posts on the FactSutra Facebook Page, and to read and reply to comments on that Page.

## Data we collect
We do not collect, store, sell or share personal data from Facebook users. Public comments on our posts may be read solely to moderate or respond to them.

## Third parties
We do not share any data with third parties.

## Data deletion
FactSutra does not store personal data from Facebook users. To request removal of any data or comments, email **thefactsutranews@proton.me** with the subject "Data deletion request" and we will respond within 30 days.

You can also remove the app's access in Facebook under **Settings → Apps and Websites**.

## Contact
Questions about this policy: **thefactsutranews@proton.me**
